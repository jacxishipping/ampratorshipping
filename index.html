FAQ

Clear answers before you ship.

The common questions customers ask before moving vehicles from the USA and Canada to Afghanistan.

Which route does Amprator use for Afghanistan vehicle shipping?

Amprator coordinates vehicle shipping from across the USA and Canada to Afghanistan using one of two route options: through Mersin, Turkey or through UAE. The team then supports final customs and delivery inside Afghanistan.

Can you deliver outside Herat or Kabul?

Yes. Herat is a primary hub, and the team supports delivery planning across all 34 Afghan provinces including Kabul, Kandahar, Mazar-i-Sharif, Jalalabad, Kunduz, and other destinations.

What details are needed for an accurate quote?

Vehicle year, make, model, pickup location, destination province, title or auction details, vehicle condition, and any timing constraints help the team confirm the most accurate route and price.

Does the quote form change any shipment or dashboard data?

No. The public quote form only sends your request to the Amprator team. It does not create a dashboard account, shipment record, or database workflow by itself.

Can I track a shipment after it is active?

Yes. The public site links to shipment tracking, and active customers can receive milestone updates as the vehicle moves through pickup, port, transit, customs, and delivery stages.
